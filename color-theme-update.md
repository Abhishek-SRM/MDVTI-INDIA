## Aesthetic Considerations

The new color scheme creates:
- **Warmth & Energy**: Burgundy red conveys passion and vitality
- **Sophistication**: Navy blue adds depth and professionalism  
- **Comfort & Accessibility**: Warm beige provides soft contrast
- **Premium Feel**: The combination feels luxurious and inviting
- **Yoga Brand Alignment**: Earthy tones connect to traditional yoga aesthetics

All colors maintain proper contrast ratios for accessibility while creating a cohesive, premium visual experience.
