About the Program

Course Overview

The Post Graduate Diploma in Yoga Teacher (PGDYT) is an intensive 1-year course designed to transform enthusiasts into well-rounded, competent, and highly skilled professional yoga teachers.

This program combines theoretical knowledge with practical training to equip you with everything needed to teach yoga effectively and establish a successful career in the field. Our curriculum is structured to meet modern teaching standards while honoring traditional yoga philosophy.

You'll learn anatomy, teaching methodology, therapy techniques, and business skills — everything required to become a sought-after yoga instructor in today's market.
